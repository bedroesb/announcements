services_affected:

  - rdm-portal
summary: >-
  Project volumes are archived twelve months after a project ends. Group
  leaders receive a notification before anything is moved.
---

From 1 July 2026 onwards, project volumes are moved to the archive tier twelve
months after the project end date recorded in the RDM Portal.

Archived data stays retrievable on request, with a restore time of one to two
working days. Group leaders receive a notification 30 days before a volume is
archived.
